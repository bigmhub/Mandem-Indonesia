Rinjani Day 1 — ascent to crater rim
Sembalun → grasslands → cloud forest → 2,639m
Rinjani D1
+
06:00
Transfer to Sembalun trailhead — register at RIC
Sembalun Lawang 1,156m. Meet guides and porters. Briefing on route, safety and environmental rules.
08:00
Begin ascent — open grassland, Pos 1 → Pos 2 → Pos 3
Gentle start through cattle grazing land. Casuarina forest begins. Wild macaque monkeys visible.
16:00

Evening
Dinner at the rim — best stargazing of the trip
Zero light pollution at 2,600m. Sleep early — 03:00 alarm tomorrow.
Pack for the rim: Warm jacket (5-10°C at night), gloves, hat, headlamp, tiger balm for muscles. The porters carry the tents, food and heavy gear.
25Aug · Tue
Rinjani Day 2 — summit · lake · hot springs
3,726m summit · Segara Anak crater lake · geothermal springs
Rinjani D2Summit
+
03:00
Summit push begins — 3hrs on volcanic scree
Steep, exposed, cold. Optional but almost everyone does it. The summit at 3,726m with the sunrise is the single best moment of the trip.
06:00
Summit sunrise — Bali's Agung visible on the horizon
Segara Anak crater lake below, Sumbawa behind you. The Wallace Line visible from the top of Indonesia.
Morning
Descend to crater lake Segara Anak — 2,000m
2-3hrs descent on steep trail. The lake is extraordinary — Mt Baru active volcano sits in the middle of it.
Afternoon
Swim in hot springs — geothermal healing waters
Pilgrimage hot springs believed to have healing powers. Locals come specifically for these. Completely surreal.
Late PM
Climb to Senaru crater rim — Plawangan I, 2,461m
2.5hrs up from the lake. Second camp at the rim. Dinner, recovery, sleep.
