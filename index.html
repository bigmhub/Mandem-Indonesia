The Brief

Surf. Safari. Perahera.
Two weeks, one island.

Eight days of private surf instruction, a two-day leopard safari at Yala, the Kandy Esala Perahera — one of Asia's greatest festivals — Sigiriya Rock Fortress, cloud forest trekking through the Knuckles, deep sea fishing, scuba diving and cliff jumping. One villa in Arugam Bay. Everything else radiates out.

01 / 06Arrival & OrientationDays 1–2+
Day 1
Land Colombo —
transfer south
Morning
Land at Bandaranaike International, Colombo
Have your e-visa printed or downloaded. Never drive to A-Bay at night.
Midday
Private 10-seat minivan to Arugam Bay — ~6.5hrs
Pre-book through your villa host. Stop in Monaragala for food.
Evening
Arrive villa — first beach walk
Eat at Sooriya Restaurant or Gecko Cafe. Both excellent seafood within walking distance.
Book in advance: A 10-seat van from Colombo costs ~£80–100 total. Arrange through the villa before you fly.
Day 2
First surf +
explore A-Bay
Morning
Walk to Main Point — just watch the break first
Read the waves before you paddle. See where locals sit. This is how you learn a break.
09:30
First instructor session at Baby Point — 2hrs
Sand pop-up practice first, then white water only. Set expectations for the week.
Afternoon
Explore A-Bay — rent motorbikes for the trip
~£5–8 per bike per day. Non-negotiable. You'll use them for everything.
Evening
Group dinner — Hideaway Restaurant
Best crab curry in Arugam Bay. Order the coconut rice. Sit outside.
02 / 06Surf FoundationDays 3–5+
Day 3
Surf +
Elephant Rock
09:30
Instructor session — Baby Point / Pottuvil Point
Focus: consistent pop-up, riding whitewater end-to-end, reading waves.
Afternoon
Motorbike to Elephant Rock — cliff jumping
~20 min ride. 5m, 8m, 12m jumps into the lagoon. Bring a waterproof bag.
16:30
Optional late afternoon free surf at sunset
Wind drops, conditions clean back up. Best light of the day.
Evening
Villa pool — rest and food
Surfing in 30°C heat is deceptive. Rest without guilt.
Day 4
Surf + Kudumbigala
jungle monastery
09:30
Instructor session — first green wave attempts
This is the breakthrough session. Instructors push you into unbroken waves for the first time.
Afternoon
Motorbike to Kudumbigala ancient jungle monastery
2,000 year old rock monastery buried in the jungle. Completely deserted. Climb the rocks at sunset for extraordinary views. One of the most atmospheric places in eastern Sri Lanka.
Sunset
Pottuvil lagoon sunset drinks
Bring beers from the shop on the way back. Crocodiles visible from the bank.
Day 5
Free surf +
deep sea fishing
Morning
Free surf — no instructor, consolidate what you've learnt
Some will want a rest morning. Let them.
13:00
Deep sea fishing charter — 4hrs offshore
Book through local fishermen at the harbour. Target: yellowfin tuna, mahi mahi, barracuda.
Evening
Your catch cooked at Stardust Beach Hotel
They'll cook whatever you bring. Best fish BBQ in A-Bay. Eat outside on the beach.
Negotiate as a group: A half-day charter should be £80–120 total, not per person.
03 / 06Yala SafariDays 6–7+
Day 6
Drive to Yala —
afternoon game drive
Morning
Leisurely breakfast, pack for overnight
10:00
Drive to Yala — ~2hrs
Pick up boxed lunches from A-Bay to eat en route.
12:00
Check into Yala Safari Camping — lunch at camp
Website: srilankayalasafari.com — book 2–3 months ahead for August.
14:00
Afternoon game drive — 2pm to 6pm with Daya Safari
Bronze package. Animals active at water sources as heat drops. Best chance of sloth bear sightings. Split into 2 jeeps.
Evening
Dinner at camp around the fire 🔥
Communal fire, cold beers, debrief the drive. Early night — 5am tomorrow.
Daya Safari contact: dayasafari.com — Bronze package $412 for 8 people per drive (all-inclusive inc. park fees, drinks, binoculars). Book 2 jeeps.
Day 7
Dawn drive 🐆 —
return to A-Bay
05:00
Wake up — jeeps depart at 5:30am
The most important alarm of the entire trip. Leopards are most active at dawn. Do not sleep through it.
05:30
Dawn game drive — Daya Safari Bronze package
First jeeps into the park. Peak leopard activity. The best wildlife window of the entire two weeks.
10:00
Back to camp — breakfast, check out
12:00
Drive back to Arugam Bay via Tissamaharama
Stop for rice and curry in Tissa. Best local lunch on the route.
Evening
Back at villa — group debrief over beers
Everyone will have a leopard story. One of the great evenings of the trip.
04 / 06Surf Progression,
Scuba & Free DayDays 8–10+
Day 8
Green wave milestone +
swimming elephants 🐘
09:30
Instructor session — green wave milestone
Bodies rested after the safari break. This is when progression clicks. Focus: wave reading, lineup positioning, unassisted paddling.
16:30
Pottuvil Lagoon at sunset — wild elephants swimming 🐘
One of only two places in Sri Lanka where you can watch wild elephants swim across the lagoon. 15-min tuk tuk from the villa. Free. Don't miss it.
Day 9
Surf + scuba +
first big night out 🍻
09:30
Morning instructor session
Stringing 2–3 rides per session now. Push towards consistent unassisted paddling.
14:00
Scuba intro dive off Arugam Bay
2-tank dive off the point. Reef fish, rays, turtles. No experience needed for an intro dive.
Evening
First big night out — Whiskey Point
Beach party atmosphere. Better crowd and setting than the main strip.
Day 10
Free day —
no agenda
Morning
Sleep in — no alarm, no instructor, no plan
Every lads trip needs a day like this.
Afternoon
Motorbike south — Panama village + Panama Beach
One of the oldest inhabited villages in Sri Lanka. Empty beach, local food, zero tourists. 20 mins from A-Bay.
16:30
Optional sunset surf session
Wind drops late afternoon. Some of the best free surf sessions happen now.
Evening
Easy villa night — order food in, early-ish bed
Big couple of days coming up.
05 / 06Kandy Perahera,
Sigiriya & KnucklesDays 11–12+
Day 11
Drive to Kandy —
Esala Perahera 🐘
08:00
Drive A-Bay to Kandy — ~3.5hrs
Stop at a village kade en route for hoppers and egg curry. Under £1pp.
12:00
Arrive Kandy — check in, explore town
Walk the lake, visit the Temple of the Tooth, eat at a local restaurant on the Kandy strip.
Evening
Kandy Esala Perahera procession 🐘🔥
One of the greatest spectacles in Asia. Beautifully decorated elephants, fire dancers, drummers and monks parading through candlelit streets. Runs August 23–27 in 2026. This is the cultural centrepiece of the entire trip.
Night
Overnight in Kandy
Stay central — within walking distance of the procession route.
Perahera timing: The Final Randoli Perahera on August 27th is the most spectacular night. Time your overnight here accordingly. Get there early and find a good vantage point on the street.
Day 12
Sigiriya Rock +
Knuckles jungle trek
08:00
Drive Kandy to Sigiriya — ~2hrs
Straight after breakfast. Arrive before the midday heat.
10:30
Sigiriya Rock Fortress — allow 2.5hrs
1,200 steps to the summit of a 200m volcanic rock. 5th century royal palace at the top. Views across the jungle are extraordinary. Entry £22pp. Watch for hornets near the mirror wall.
14:00
Drive to Knuckles Mountain Range — ~1.5hrs
South through Matale. Tea plantations line the road before you leave the van.
15:30
Mini World's End → Corbett's Gap jungle trek — 2–3hrs
Dense cloud forest, waterfalls, river crossings, cliff edge views. Pre-arranged local guide essential — £15–20 total for the group. Tuck trousers into socks for leeches.
18:00
Waterfall swim at end of trail
Cold freshwater after a full day of adventures. One of the best moments of the trip.
19:00
Drive back to Arugam Bay — ~3.5hrs
Arrive A-Bay ~22:30. Long day. Easy night in.
Guide booking: Contact Knuckles Adventure Trekking or arrange through your villa host before you leave A-Bay. £15–20 total for the group — not per person.
06 / 06Final Surf &
Send-offDays 13–14+
Day 13
Best surf day +
send-off night 🍻
09:30
Final instructor session — push to the ceiling 🎥
Instructors push everyone to their limit. Record everything. Book a local surf photographer for this session — ~£25 for the group.
Midday
Villa afternoon — pool, pack, debrief
Last afternoon at the pool. Share photos and footage. Order food in.
16:30
One last sunset free surf
Optional but highly recommended. Just go out and enjoy it without thinking about technique.
Evening
Send-off dinner + final big night out
Best restaurant you found across the trip. Then drinks on the beach. This is the night.
Day 14
Last free surf —
fly home ✈️
07:30
Final free surf — no instructor
Just the group and the waves and what you've built. Don't skip it.
09:00
Minivan departs for Colombo — 6.5hrs
Tip your villa staff and instructors generously. They made the trip.
En route
Stop in Colombo — Pettah Market + Ministry of Crab
Last-minute shopping at Pettah. Ministry of Crab for the final meal — one of Asia's great seafood restaurants. Book ahead.
Evening
Fly home from Bandaranaike International
